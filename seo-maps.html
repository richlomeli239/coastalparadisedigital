Search Engine Optimization & Digital Maps

Rank higher on Google, Apple Maps, and Microsoft Bing so nearby, national, and international customers find you before your competitors.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) means making sure search engines understand what you do and show your business when customers search. Whether you serve local Southwest Florida neighborhoods or want to reach customers nationally and internationally, we make sure you rank high and get noticed.

Google Maps & Apple Maps Visibility

Millions of drivers and smartphone users search for local businesses on Apple Maps and Google Maps every day. We optimize your map pins, business category data, and address signals so you appear at the top of map search results.

National & International Targeting

Not all businesses are limited to one neighborhood. If you sell products, offer nationwide services, or cater to international travelers and second-home owners visiting Southwest Florida, we expand your SEO footprint to capture global searchers.

Microsoft Bing Search Visibility

Microsoft Bing is the default search engine on Windows PCs across offices and homes. We optimize your website specifically for Bing Webmaster guidelines so you capture the high-income demographic that uses Microsoft devices.

Clean Directory Listings & Site Speed

Inconsistent business names or old phone numbers across web directories confuse search engines and hurt your rankings. We clean up your public listings and optimize your website speed so pages load instantly on all mobile phones.
