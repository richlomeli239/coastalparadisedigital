The Retail Trap vs. Agency Procurement

Why Businesses Lose Money on Direct Media Buys

When you call local television stations, radio conglomerates, or outdoor billboard operators directly, their sales reps quote you standard rate cards. They push remaining, off-peak inventory that benefits their commissions, not your bottom line.

As your designated media buyer, Coastal Paradise Digital works exclusively for you. We use institutional media procurement software (STRATA) to cross-reference Nielsen rating points, analyze cost-per-point (CPP) and cost-per-thousand (CPM), and negotiate fierce rate concessions—typically saving clients 15% to 25% while securing prime dayparts and high-traffic billboard locations.

The Institutional Procurement Process

How we protect your budget and maximize audience reach from initial proposal to post-buy invoice audit.

STEP 01

Audience Modeling & Nielsen Analysis

We define your high-value customer profile and evaluate quantitative Nielsen and Scarborough data to find the exact TV programs, radio stations, and transit routes your audience consumes.

STEP 02

Competitive Vendor RFP & Rate Bidding

We pit local media vendors against one another in competitive blind bidding, demanding make-goods, bonus commercial spots, and wholesale CPP rates that single businesses cannot obtain alone.

STEP 03

Execution & Server-Side Attribution

We sync broadcast flight dates with dynamic call tracking (CallRail) and server-side digital spikes, enabling real-time visibility into which commercials triggered website visits and inbound calls.

STEP 04

Post-Buy Audit & Credit Recovery

We audit every station log and invoice. If a commercial aired at 2:00 AM instead of 6:00 PM, or a billboard was obscured, we demand dollar-for-dollar cash credits or prime-time make-goods.
