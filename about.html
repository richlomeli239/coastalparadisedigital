The Story Behind Coastal Paradise Digital

Rich Lomeli’s career began in high-reliability computing and automated data processing with the United States Navy (NARDAC), maintaining mission-critical readiness and strict security compliance. That foundational operational discipline became the cornerstone of a 15-year career directing multi-million-dollar advertising portfolios.

At Spectrum Reach, Rich served as Enterprise Manager for Paid Digital Search & Social Media, governing over $30M+ in annual multi-channel spend across Google, Meta, and Microsoft. He led the national rollout of their paid social media offerings, increasing category revenue by over 300% in 5 years and authoring training curriculum presented to 3,000+ sales associates nationwide.

Earlier in Fort Myers at Priority Marketing and in Omaha at Swanson Russell, Rich negotiated, executed, and audited traditional and digital media buys (TV, Radio, Print, OOH, Search) exceeding $10M–$30M+ using STRATA and Nielsen ratings data. Today, as founder of Coastal Paradise Digital and publicist/webmaster for American Legion Post 90 in Cape Coral, Rich brings that exact enterprise rigor to businesses across Southwest Florida.
