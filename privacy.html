1. Introduction

At Coastal Paradise Digital ("we," "our," or "us"), we value and respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit coastalparadisedigital.com or contact our team, and how we protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information You Provide: When you fill out our contact form, call us, or email us, you provide information such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, website URL, and details about your project.
  • Standard Website Analytics: Like most websites, we collect basic technical information such as browser type, general geographic area (such as Southwest Florida), and pages visited, to help us improve our website experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to:

We implement strict technical and organizational safeguards to ensure your information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/SSL) and stored securely.
