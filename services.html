Answers in Plain English

Services FAQ

Everything you need to know about our services, process, and results for Southwest Florida businesses.

How do I know which digital marketing service my business needs?

If you need new customer calls immediately, Google and Microsoft Bing search ads are typically the best option because your ads show up at the very moment someone searches for your service. If you want sustainable long-term phone calls without paying for every single click, Local SEO and Google Maps optimization are ideal. If your website looks outdated or fails to turn visitors into phone calls, a modern custom redesign is your top priority.

Can I combine multiple services into one package?

Yes. Many small businesses combine website design with Google Ads and Local SEO for a complete lead generation system. We build customized, affordable packages based on your specific business goals and monthly budget.

Do you handle drone videography and graphic design in-house?

Yes. We capture 4K aerial drone footage and create professional graphic designs right here in Southwest Florida. We produce stunning visual assets for your website, YouTube channel, social media ads, TV commercials, and printed banners or mailers.

How do we track the results of our marketing campaigns?

We provide straightforward monthly reporting focused on real business metrics: phone calls generated, form submissions received, and new customers gained. No confusing technical jargon—just clear proof of your return on investment.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work on flexible month-to-month retainers. We believe in earning your business every single month through measurable phone calls, new customers, and dedicated local support.
