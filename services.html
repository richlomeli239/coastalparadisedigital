PILLAR 10 Arriving Q2 2027 CompTIA Network+

Enterprise Networking, Systems Administration & Cloud Infrastructure

Pre-Scope 2027 Infrastructure
Plain English Summary: Modern business operations cannot tolerate dropped VoIP calls, laggy point-of-sale systems, or local server crashes. Once coursework is completed and certified in CompTIA Network+, we design, build, and maintain rock-solid computer networks, structured wiring topologies, and cloud server environments—guaranteeing 24/7 uptime, maximum throughput, and zero bottlenecks for your team and customers.

What We Will Deliver (Upon Certification & Completion):

  • Enterprise Network Topology & Architecture: Designing resilient wired and wireless local area networks (LAN/WLAN), structured cabling backbones, IPv4/IPv6 subnetting, and dynamic routing (OSPF, BGP).
  • VLAN Segmentation & Traffic Isolation: Segmenting guest Wi-Fi, point-of-sale (POS) terminals, VoIP communication, and confidential business databases onto isolated Virtual LANs to eliminate lateral security risks.
  • Cloud & Hybrid Server Administration: Provisioning, configuring, and maintaining virtualized server environments across AWS, Google Cloud (GCP), Microsoft Azure, and on-premise Windows/Linux server stacks.
  • Commercial Switch, Router & Access Point Provisioning: Hands-on staging, deployment, and firmware lifecycle management of enterprise-grade managed switches, multi-WAN failover routers, and Wi-Fi 6/7 access points.
  • Site-to-Site & Remote Access VPN Tunnels: Engineering encrypted IPsec and WireGuard tunnels and Software-Defined WAN (SD-WAN) frameworks for secure multi-branch and remote worker connectivity.
  • Network Health Diagnostics & Packet Telemetry: Performing deep packet inspection (Wireshark), Quality of Service (QoS) prioritization for voice/video, bandwidth optimization, and automated failover recovery.

The Operational & Strategic Advantage:

High-converting digital advertising and customer acquisition engines are only as dependable as the network infrastructure supporting them. When we architect your customer-facing digital applications or CRM workflows, we ensure the underlying physical and cloud networks possess the speed, throughput, and failover redundancy required for zero-downtime commerce.

Academic & Credential Milestone: Built on DSDT's accredited networking, server administration, and cloud curriculum; targeting CompTIA Network+ certification in Q2 2027. Early planning consultations available for late-2027 corporate rollouts.
PILLAR 11 Arriving Q2 2027 CompTIA Security+

Enterprise Cybersecurity, Threat Defense & Risk Management

Pre-Scope 2027 Security
Plain English Summary: Ransomware, wire fraud, and customer data breaches can bankrupt a business overnight. Once coursework is completed and certified in CompTIA Security+, we lock down your corporate perimeter with defense-in-depth safeguards, enforce zero-trust identity controls, harden firewalls, and ensure your proprietary data complies with strict regulatory standards.

What We Will Deliver (Upon Certification & Completion):

  • Zero-Trust Identity & Access Management (IAM): Architecting least-privilege role-based access controls (RBAC), mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and Single Sign-On (SSO) across all cloud apps and devices.
  • Perimeter Defense & Next-Gen Firewalls (NGFW): Deploying stateful packet inspection, intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS), DNS filtering, and secure web gateways to block unauthorized intrusion attempts.
  • Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR): Hardening workstations, mobile devices, and servers against ransomware, polymorphic malware, zero-day payloads, and unauthorized script execution.
  • Cryptographic Data Protection: Enforcing end-to-end data encryption for data-at-rest (AES-256) and data-in-transit (TLS 1.3), complete with certificate lifecycle management and secure key vaults.
  • Regulatory Governance & Compliance Auditing: Implementing technical controls aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), ISO/IEC 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, and SOC 2 Type II trust principles.
  • Incident Response & Business Continuity Playbooks: Establishing disaster recovery runbooks, immutable air-gapped backups, containment protocols, and workforce anti-phishing simulations.

The Operational & Strategic Advantage:

Customer databases, billing telemetry, and employee credentials are the primary targets of modern cyber adversaries. By engineering defense-in-depth directly into your operating model, we insulate your balance sheet and customer trust against extortion, operational paralysis, and crippling legal liabilities.

Academic & Credential Milestone: Grounded in DSDT's comprehensive cybersecurity and server hardening curriculum; targeting CompTIA Security+ certification in Q2 2027. Early compliance scoping available for 2027 client roadmaps.
PILLAR 12 Arriving Q2 2027 CompTIA CySA+

Cybersecurity Analysis, Threat Intelligence & Penetration Testing

Pre-Scope 2027 CySA+
Plain English Summary: The only way to know if your defenses actually work is to test them the same way a real hacker would. Once coursework is completed and certified in CompTIA CySA+ (Cybersecurity Analyst), we perform authorized ethical hacking (penetration testing) to expose security vulnerabilities before malicious actors exploit them, and deploy behavioral threat hunting tools to continuously monitor and neutralize active cyber threats.

What We Will Deliver (Upon Certification & Completion):

  • Authorized Penetration Testing & Ethical Hacking: Methodical simulated cyber attacks against public-facing web applications, network firewalls, APIs, and cloud infrastructure to identify exploitable security gaps.
  • Continuous Vulnerability Scanning & CVSS Scoring: Automated and manual vulnerability assessments using industry-standard scanners, categorizing CVE exposures and delivering prioritized remediation roadmaps.
  • Proactive Threat Hunting & Behavioral Analytics: Analyzing network telemetry and system behavior to identify Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs), credential dumping, zero-day payloads, and unauthorized lateral movement.
  • SIEM & SOAR Telemetry Architecture: Deploying Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) pipelines to aggregate server, firewall, and endpoint logs, paired with automated SOAR incident containment rules.
  • Digital Forensics & Threat Eradication: Forensic memory and disk artifact extraction, attack timeline reconstruction, threat isolation, root-cause analysis, and post-breach mitigation documentation.
  • Defensive Engineering & Threat Intelligence Feeds: Integrating real-time threat intelligence feeds into firewall rulesets to proactively block emerging global attacker infrastructure before it targets your systems.

The Operational & Strategic Advantage:

Passive security tools only alert you after an alert fires; CySA+ analytical tradecraft hunts down hidden threats before damage is done. By combining ethical offensive penetration testing with continuous behavioral analytics, we ensure mid-market and enterprise organizations maintain an active, battle-tested cyber posture.

Academic & Credential Milestone: Culminating from DSDT's intensive penetration testing, threat hunting, and cyber analyst laboratory program; targeting CompTIA CySA+ certification in Q2 2027. Early priority testing waitlist open for 2027 engagements.
